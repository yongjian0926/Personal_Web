A Letter of Gratitude on My 20th Birthday

September 26, 2026

September 26, 2026 marks my 20th birthday, a milestone as I officially step into adulthood. Looking back on the journey so far, my heart is filled with immense gratitude.

First and foremost, I want to express my deepest gratitude to my parents. For the past 20 years, your unconditional love, selfless support, and sacrifices have shaped me into who I am today. From my earliest steps to every major decision, you have always been my stronghold. Thank you for not only giving me life, but also teaching me to face the world with courage and kindness. No matter how far I go, your love will always be my safest home.

I would also like to express my heartfelt thanks to my dear friend, Tian Chenshuo. During these university years—a time full of challenges and growth—I feel so fortunate to have you by my side. Thank you for your constant encouragement whenever I felt uncertain, and for all the unforgettable moments we’ve shared. Your support has made my college life so much richer and given me the confidence to keep moving forward.

Turning 20 is a brand-new beginning. Carrying my parents' love and expectation, along with the steadfast support of a great friend, I look forward to embracing the future with bravery and optimism.

Thank you all for always being there for me.

— Yongjian Wang
